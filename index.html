Meshchat neXt Privacy Policy

Meshchat neXt is developed by HK1200. For privacy questions, contact hk1200.dev@gmail.com.

The app creates or imports a Reticulum identity and stores the identity, settings, contacts and message history on the device. When you use messaging, voice, shared rooms or File Sync, the app sends the information necessary for that feature to the peers, relays, room servers, companion radios or SIP services you configure. That information can include your display name, addressing information, message text, files, photos, and live call audio. Other people in a shared room can see what you post there. Location sharing sends the position you choose to share. Map downloads and configured network connections can expose network metadata such as your IP address to their operators.

Android permissions let you use your microphone for calls, your camera for QR codes, Bluetooth for compatible radios, and location for location features. Permissions and service settings can be managed on the device. Network and transport protections vary by protocol and service configuration. Treat any information shared with a room, peer or external provider as information that recipient can retain.

Local data remains on the device until it is deleted through an available app control or by clearing the app's Android storage. Clearing app storage also removes locally stored settings and identities. Any copies already sent to other people or services, or exported by you, are managed separately.
