Meshchat neXt Privacy Policy

Meshchat neXt is developed by HK1200. For privacy questions, contact hk1200.dev@gmail.com.

The app creates or imports a Reticulum identity and stores the identity, settings, contacts and message history on the device. When you use messaging, voice, shared rooms or File Sync, the app sends the information necessary for that feature to the peers, relays, room servers, companion radios or SIP services you configure. That information can include your display name, addressing information, message text, files, photos, and live call audio. Other people in a shared room can see what you post there. Location sharing sends the position you choose to share. Map downloads and configured network connections can expose network metadata such as your IP address to their operators.

System permissions let you use your microphone for calls, your camera for QR codes, Bluetooth for compatible radios, and location for location features. Permissions and service settings can be managed on the device. Network and transport protections vary by protocol and service configuration. Treat any information shared with a room, peer or external provider as information that recipient can retain.

Local data remains on the device until it is deleted through an available app control or by clearing the app’s local storage. Clearing app storage also removes locally stored settings and identities. Any copies already sent to other people or services, or exported by you, are managed separately.

Optional mailbox alerts

neXt does not choose or enable an alert provider automatically. If you configure a recipient’s alert route, neXt can send a signed hint to that HTTPS provider after the selected relay accepts encrypted mail. The hint contains public routing addresses, an encrypted-message identifier, a timestamp and signature. It does not contain message text, file/photo contents or location coordinates.

Compatible push-enabled iOS development builds can also register to receive alerts. This is optional and requires an explicitly chosen provider, enrollment code and approved senders. That provider receives the Apple push token, public Reticulum address and approved senders’ public keys. Apple receives the push token and a generic alert without a contact name or message content. Personal-signed previews cannot receive live Apple push alerts.

Turning receiving alerts off completes after the provider acknowledges removal; offline removal remains pending until it can be retried. The current reference provider removes active registration data on acknowledgment and expires delivery metadata after about one hour while running. Other providers and operator-managed logs, configuration and backups may have different retention. Turning alerts off does not delete encrypted mail held by a relay or copies received by other people.
