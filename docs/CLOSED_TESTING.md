# Join the Android closed tests

Join each app separately with the Google account you use in the Play Store.
Your email must already be on the approved tester list. These are the closed-test
links to share:

- [Meshchat neXt](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.haadkoe.meshchatnext)
- [neXt Family](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/org.meshchat.family)

## Join and install

1. Open the appropriate link in a browser and check the Google account shown at
   the top. It must match the account on the tester list and in your Play Store.
2. Choose **Become a tester** if offered. If the page says **You are a tester**,
   you have already joined that program.
3. Follow **download it on Google Play** and choose **Install** or **Update**.
   Repeat for the other app if you are testing both.
4. Keep the closed-test enrollment active and use the app normally. Report what
   happened, the app version, phone model and Android version when you find a
   problem. Send private logs or account details directly to
   **hk1200.dev@gmail.com**, rather than posting them in a public issue.

Do not uninstall an existing app or clear its data just to enroll. The signed
Play builds support updating compatible existing installations in place. If you
already have the latest build, Play may not offer an Update button. Keep your
settings; do not force an update by deleting the app.

## If you previously joined an internal test

Internal testing and closed testing are different programs. Google says an
internal tester must leave the internal test before joining the closed test.
An earlier invitation containing `/apps/internaltest/` is an internal-test link;
the two links above use `/apps/testing/` for the closed tests.

Open the earlier internal invitation. If it confirms you are enrolled in that
internal test, leave that program, then return to the appropriate closed-test
link above and join it. If it offers **Accept invite**, do not accept that
internal invitation when your goal is to join the closed test.

Do not leave and rejoin an existing closed test to troubleshoot an update.
Each app needs at least 12 testers enrolled continuously for 14 days before a
new personal developer account can apply for production access. Adding an email
to the developer's list does not enroll its owner; each tester must opt in.

If the link says the app is unavailable, check the signed-in Google account and
whether you are still enrolled in the earlier internal test. Ask
**hk1200.dev@gmail.com** to check your approved email if the problem remains.

Google documents the [testing programs](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334?hl=en)
and [production testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465).
